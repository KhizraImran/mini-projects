Our Story

Where Every Dish Tells a Story

Nestled along 107 Street in the heart of Al Farwaniyah, Food Story has been serving the people of Kuwait since 2023. We are a casual, family-friendly restaurant where the aromas of whole spices, slow-cooked biryanis, and freshly baked breads welcome you at the door.

Our kitchen brings together three beloved cuisines under one roof: authentic Indian curries and tandoor specials, Chinese favorites with a desi twist, and Arabic grills and rice platters. This wide range makes us a gathering spot for Indian expats, Kuwaiti families, and workers looking for generous portions at honest prices.
