60 Years of Egyptian Flavour

Born in Alexandria.
Loved Across the Gulf.

GAD was founded in 1963 at Raml Station, Alexandria — what started as a beloved neighbourhood spot became Egypt's most recognised street food name, built on the simplest, most honest cooking in the world.

Today, GAD operates branches across Kuwait, UAE, Qatar, and Saudi Arabia — carrying the same recipes, the same sesame bread, the same commitment to honest Egyptian cooking that made it legendary.

The Farwaniya branch on Habeeb Munawer Street serves the community from early morning until midnight, seven days a week — breakfast for the early riser, dinner for the night owl, and everything in between.
