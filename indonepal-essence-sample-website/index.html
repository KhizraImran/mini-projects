Our Story

Where India Meets the Himalayas

Born from a love of authentic home cooking, IndoNepal Essence brings together the bold spices of North India and the comforting staples of Nepal. Every dish is prepared fresh in our Jleeb, Farwaniya kitchen using recipes passed down through generations.

From hand-folded momos to slow-cooked curries and fragrant biryanis, we serve food that feels like home — whether you dine in, take away or order delivery across Kuwait.
