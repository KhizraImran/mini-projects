Our Story

Where Three Culinary Worlds Meet

Trinity Spice sits inside the lively Lulu Hypermarket Food Court in Al-Dajeej, Farwaniya. The name reflects our three pillars: the slow-cooked gravies of the North, the spice-laced rice of the South, and the charcoal-kissed mandi traditions of Arabia. Every plate is built for families, workers, and friends who want generous portions at honest Kuwaiti prices.
