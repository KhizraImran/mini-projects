Our Kitchen

The Flavors of Kerala, Right Here in Farwaniya

Green Pepper was born from a simple belief — that people living far from home deserve food that actually tastes like home. Every dish comes from Kerala kitchen tradition: whole spices, slow cooking, real coconut oil.

Whether it's a plate of Jeerakasala biryani layered with tender beef, or a humble bowl of Kappa with curry, Green Pepper delivers the kind of food that makes you forget you're in Kuwait.
